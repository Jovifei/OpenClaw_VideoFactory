## Analysis policy

PySceneDetect `0.7.1` uses `ContentDetector` threshold `27` and a minimum
scene length of `0.8` seconds. The report contains scene times, representative
time points, median shot duration, density, and a three-level pace label. A
complete cached faster-whisper `1.2.1` small snapshot is loaded in CPU `int8`
offline mode; missing cache or no audio records `unavailable` and does not
block the original render.
